# Elara Pricing and Operating Model (Draft v1)

## Goal
Define a clear commercial model for Elara:
- Public users receive realtime signals.
- Internal core team uses full execution features.
- Keep growth-friendly packaging without delayed signals.

## Product Positioning
Elara is operated in two lanes:

1. Public lane (signal-only)
- Realtime signals.
- No public auto-execution.
- No public access to broker execution controls.

2. Core lane (internal desk, max 4 users)
- Full execution features.
- Risk override with strict controls.
- Internal-only auto-execution rights.

## Subscription Tiers (No Delay)
All tiers receive signals in realtime. Differentiation is based on monthly signal credits and signal depth.

| Tier | Price | Signal Credits / Month | Speed | Included Signal Detail |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Free | Rp0 | 10 | Realtime | Entry + SL + TP1, major symbols only |
| Reguler | Rp149,000 | 20 | Realtime | Entry + SL + TP1, A-tier setups |
| Premium | Rp599,000 | 80 | Realtime | Entry ladder + SL + TP1/TP2 + invalidation updates |
| Super Premium | Rp1,990,000 | 250 (fair use) | Realtime | Full setup + full intraday updates + priority support |

## Credit Rules
1. Credits reset monthly.
2. No rollover to next month.
3. Credits are consumed when a signal is delivered.
4. Free tier does not include full intraday management updates.

## Internal Risk Policy (Core Lane)
1. Max portfolio drawdown cap: 10%.
2. Per-person allocation starts at 25% each (for 4 users).
3. Per-trade risk cap: 0.35% of each internal allocation.
4. Daily loss cap per person: 1.2%.
5. Weekly loss cap per person: 2.5%.
6. Monthly loss cap per person: 4.0%.
7. Breach action: account lock, then two-person approval required to resume.

## Hard Safety Rules
1. Public users cannot trigger execution commands.
2. Critical actions must be audited (who, when, what).
3. Resume or risk override must require secondary approval.
4. Global kill-switch remains available for emergency shutdown.

## Compliance Note
This model is designed for:
- public signal distribution, and
- private internal execution.

If managing third-party funds directly, legal and regulatory review is required before go-live.

## Rollout Plan
1. Publish this model publicly as product documentation.
2. Launch Free + Reguler first.
3. Add Premium and Super Premium after support workflow is stable.
4. Keep auto-execution restricted to internal desk only.
